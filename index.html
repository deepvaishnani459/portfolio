About me

I am Dr. Deep Vaishnani (MBBS), a physician, medical researcher, and AI in healthcare specialist working at the intersection of clinical medicine, biomedical research, and data-driven innovation.

I graduated in the top 2% of my cohort and have developed strong expertise across clinical practice and scientific research. I am the author of 14+ peer-reviewed publicationsindexed in SCI, PubMed, and Scopus, with research spanning artificial intelligence in healthcare, digital pathology, multi-omics, clinical diagnostics, and translational medicine.

I have successfully cleared international medical licensing examinations including FMGE (India)and AMC CAT MCQ (Australia), reflecting strong clinical competence and global medical readiness. My work focuses on integrating evidence-based medicine with AI-driven technologies to improve diagnostics, decision-making, and patient outcomes.

In addition to my clinical and research work, I serve as a Healthcare Growth Specialist and Medical Mentor, supporting medical students and professionals in research development, career strategy, and competitive examinations such as FMGE, AMC, NEET-PG, and INICET.

My core areas of expertise include artificial intelligence in healthcare, clinical research, precision medicine, evidence-based medicine, translational research, medical education, and healthcare innovation.

I am open to clinical roles, research collaborations, AI-healthcare initiatives, global fellowships, and high-impact healthcare projects, with a strong commitment to ethical, patient-centered, and innovation-driven healthcare.
